\section{Research}

\textbf{The GARCH-M Model with an Extreme-Downside Risk Premium} \hfill \textcolor{muted}{\small 2026}\\
\textcolor{muted}{\small with I.~S.~Slabolitskiy (HSE) $\cdot$ under review at \textit{Economics Letters}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Proposed an asymmetric GARCH-M model in which expected returns price ordinary and extreme negative shocks separately, with a closed-form unconditional variance.
  \item Estimated the extreme-shock threshold by chaining GEV and GPD fits on filtered residuals, cutting threshold RMSE by up to a quarter in Monte Carlo. On S\&P~500 returns, downside risk is strongly priced.
