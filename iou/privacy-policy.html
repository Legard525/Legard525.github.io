IoU — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 July 2026

IoU is a small app for tracking who owes whom between two people on a trip. This policy explains what it stores, where, and who can see it. It is written to be read, not to be waved at lawyers.

What the app stores

Only what you type into it:

  • The amounts and short notes you enter for each debt
  • The names you give a ledger and the person you share it with
  • The name you choose to show the other person when you link phones
  • The time each entry was made

The app does not ask for or collect your email, phone number, contacts, location, photos, or any device identifier for advertising. There is no sign-up form and no account to create.

Where it is stored

Everything lives on your phone. When you link with another person, entries are also synced through Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Anonymous Authentication) so both phones can stay in step. That data is stored on Google servers in the European Union (europe-west3, Frankfurt). Each phone is identified only by an anonymous Firebase ID that is not tied to any personal account.

Who can see it

A ledger is readable only by the two phones that share it, enforced by server-side security rules. Nobody else — including us — can read your entries through the app. Google processes and stores the data on our behalf as our infrastructure provider, under Google's privacy terms.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data. There is nothing to sell and no one to sell it to.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, and no third-party trackers in the app.
  • We do not build a profile of you or share data with anyone for marketing.

Deleting your data

Deleting a ledger removes it and all its entries from your phone. If the ledger was shared, your copy is removed and you leave the shared record; the other person keeps theirs. Uninstalling the app removes all local data. To have a shared record removed from the server entirely, both people should delete it, or contact us at the address below.

Children

IoU is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
