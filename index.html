Research

The GARCH-M Model with an Extreme-Downside Risk Premium

With I. S. Slabolitskiy (HSE). An asymmetric GARCH-M model in which expected returns price ordinary and extreme negative shocks separately, with a closed-form unconditional variance. The extreme-shock threshold is estimated by chaining GEV and GPD fits on filtered residuals, which cuts threshold RMSE by up to a quarter in Monte Carlo. On S&P 500 returns, downside risk is strongly priced.

GARCH-M Extreme Value Theory Monte Carlo Econometrics
Under review at Economics Letters

BSc Thesis: Neural Networks vs Traditional Calibration of the SABR Model

A Sobolev-loss neural surrogate of the Hagan formula (Tanh, ELU, GELU, SIREN) compared with classical calibration on GLD options. The surrogate matched or slightly beat the classical fit with a better-conditioned Jacobian, and a neural warm start cut iterations in the Feb–Mar 2026 high-volatility window.

PyTorch SABR Sobolev Training Options
HSE Moscow · grade 9/10
