Papyrus — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Papyrus is an app for handwritten notes and for writing on PDFs with a stylus. This policy is short because the app collects nothing.

What the app stores

Your notebooks, the PDFs you open in it, what you draw and type on them, pictures you add from your gallery, and your settings. All of it stays in the app's private storage on your device.

What leaves your device

Nothing, unless you send it yourself. Papyrus has no internet access (it does not request the permission), no account, no analytics, no crash reporting and no ads. When you share or save a PDF, or save a library backup, the file goes only where you choose to send or save it.

Permissions

Papyrus requests no permissions. Pictures are picked with Android's photo picker, which gives the app only the pictures you select. PDFs reach the app only when you open them in it.

Deleting your data

Deleting a notebook or PDF in the library removes it. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. Backups and exported files you saved elsewhere stay where you put them.

Children

Papyrus does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
